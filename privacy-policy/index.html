§1

Who this covers

GPA Sri Lanka is a GPA calculator for Sri Lankan university students — you pick your university and degree, enter module grades, and get a live GPA estimate. This policy covers the Android app (package com.scalar9.slgpa) and its web build. It's written by the person who builds the app, for the people who use it — plainly, and limited to what the app actually does.

GPA Sri Lanka is an independent app. It is not run by, endorsed by, or connected to any university, and nothing it shows or exports is an official result. §14 explains the labels the app uses, including “self-reported” and “verified”.

§2

What we collect

What we hold depends entirely on how you use the app:

If you never sign in Nothing about you reaches us. Guest results live only in your device's local app storage.
If you sign in Your name and email address, from Google. We never see your password; sign-in is handled entirely by Google.
Once you save a result The degree, major, modules and grades you enter, and the GPA calculated from them.
If you verify your university email The university email address Microsoft returns when you link your university account, and the time it was verified. See §4.
If you complete your profile Your full name, name with initials, registration number and gender (male or female), and — optionally — the day and month of your birthday. We never ask for your year of birth.
If you submit a transcript The academic details read from it — modules, grades, and the name and registration number printed on it — a fingerprint of the file, the outcome of the check, and when you made each attempt. The document itself is not stored. See §5.
If you report something For a wrong or missing course, or a problem with an item on the Opportunities board: what you reported, the reason, any note you type, and your verification level at the time.
Kept on your device only Your theme choice, whether you've seen the introduction, and which Opportunities items this device has already shown you. None of it is sent to us.

We do not collect your location, contacts, NIC number, year of birth, or advertising ID, and the app contains no advertising SDK and no third-party analytics tracker.

§3

Guest mode vs. a signed-in account

You can use the entire GPA calculator, the planner and the result export without ever signing in. As a guest, every result you save is written only to your device's local storage — it never touches our servers. Uninstall the app, or clear its storage, and it's gone.

If you sign in, your results are stored in our database instead, scoped to your account by row-level security — no other account, including other signed-in users, can read or write your rows. If you save results as a guest and later sign in, those local results are uploaded once, at that point, and nowhere else.

Even as a guest, the app downloads public catalog data (universities, degrees, modules, grading scales) from our servers. Like any internet service, our hosting provider sees the network address those requests come from.

§4

University email verification

Verification is optional, and separate from signing in. If you choose to verify, you link the Microsoft account your university issues, through a standard OAuth flow in your browser. We read the email address that Microsoft returns, check that its domain belongs to a supported Sri Lankan university, and store the address and a verification timestamp against your profile.

What this address is, and isn't, used for
  • Used to confirm which university you attend, which is shown as a status on your own profile and opens the Opportunities board (§6).
  • Never given to recruiters, advertisers, or anyone posting on the board.
  • Never used to send you email from us.

Once you complete your profile, your full name, name with initials, registration number and university are locked, so a verified profile can't later be rewritten into someone else's. If one of them is wrong, email us and we'll correct it.

§5

Transcript checks

Submitting a transcript is optional. If you do, the photo or PDF is sent to our server, which passes it to Google's Gemini API to be read into a list of modules and grades. Our own database logic — not the AI model — then compares that list against your degree's published modules and decides the outcome.

  • The file is not stored by us. We keep a fingerprint of it (so the same document can be recognised if it is submitted again), the details read from it, and the outcome.
  • If the check passes, the modules and grades read from the transcript become your checked record, shown to you under “My record”.
  • We count your attempts per hour and per day to limit abuse.
Read this before you upload
  • Your transcript may show your NIC number. We do not save it, but the whole document — including anything printed on it — is sent to Google to be read. If you can, cover or crop your NIC before uploading.
  • How Google handles the document. At the date of this policy we use the Gemini API's unpaid tier. Under Google's terms for that tier, Google may use what is sent to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. We intend to move to the paid tier, where it is not used that way, and will update this page when we do.
  • Your university. By submitting, you authorise us to confirm those results with your university, and a forged or altered document may be reported to it. At the date of this policy we have no arrangement with any university and have done neither.
  • A confirmed mismatch can lead to your account being permanently closed.
§6

Opportunities, offers and adverts

The Opportunities board lists internships, scholarships, graduate schemes, events and student offers. It opens once your university email is verified; internships also need one semester of your transcript checked. Some items are commercial — paid placements, or links we may earn a commission from — and each one says so in words on its card. At most one item in four is commercial.

How items are chosen for you
  • The app uses your university, degree, year of study and gender to choose which items you see. This includes sponsored offers and adverts.
  • The matching happens on your device. The app downloads the board and filters it locally. We do not tell an advertiser or poster who you are, what you saw, or that you matched.
  • Gender is required to complete a profile for this reason. It is limited to male or female, can be changed later, and is not shown to other users.
  • There is no advertising SDK in the app and no advertising profile of you is built or shared.

Tapping an item opens its link in your browser. From there you are on someone else's website, under their privacy policy; an affiliate link may tell that site you came from GPA Sri Lanka. An item's picture is loaded from the address its poster supplied, which involves an ordinary web request to that host.

The list of items you've already seen (used for the “New” badge) stays on your device. Reporting an item is the one action that tells us which item you looked at — the report is stored with your account so we can act on it and stop duplicates.

§7

Exporting and sharing your results

You can export a saved result as an image or a PDF. The file is built on your device and handed to your phone's own share sheet; it goes only where you choose to send it, and we do not receive a copy.

  • An export shows your name (if you're signed in and have one on your profile), your university, degree, GPA and — if you include them — your modules and grades.
  • A photo you add to an export is used on your device only. It is never uploaded to us and is discarded when you close the export sheet.
  • Every export is marked as self-reported and “Not issued by the university” (see §14). Please don't remove those marks or present an export as an official document.
§8

How we use it

  • Calculate and display your GPA and classification, using the grading scale your faculty publishes.
  • Keep your saved results in sync across the devices you're signed in on.
  • Show your verification status, and open the parts of the app that depend on it.
  • Check a transcript you submit against your degree's published module list, and keep an audit trail of that check.
  • Choose the opportunities, internships and offers you see, including sponsored offers and adverts (§6).
  • Fix the course catalog and remove bad board items when you report them.
  • Detect and prevent abuse, such as forged transcripts or one registration number claimed by two accounts.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your grades to target adverts.

§9

Sharing

We don't sell your personal data, and we don't give it to third parties for their own marketing. These outside services touch your data, strictly to make the app work:

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication and server functions. Your account data and saved results are stored on their infrastructure, under our access controls.
  • Google (sign-in) and Microsoft (university email verification) process those steps under their own privacy policies.
  • Google's Gemini API reads a transcript you submit — see §5, including how Google may use it.
  • Resend delivers an email to us when a report is filed. That email names the course or item reported; it does not contain your identity or the note you typed.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, and — only as described in §5 — to your university in connection with a transcript you submitted.

§10

Retention & deletion

Guest data lives only on your device, for as long as the app is installed and its storage isn't cleared — that's within your control at all times, no request to us needed.

A signed-in account's data — profile, verification status, saved results, checked record and reports you filed — is kept until you ask us to remove it. Self-service account deletion isn't built into the app yet; until it is, email us and we'll delete your account and its data.

What deleting your account does not remove

If you have submitted a transcript, one record of that submission is kept after your account is deleted: the academic details read from the document — including the name and registration number printed on it — and a fingerprint of the file. The link to your account is severed, but this record still identifies you. Deleting your account is not the same as deleting all of your data.

It is kept as the audit trail behind a checked-record status, so that a check we made can still be accounted for afterwards and a rejected document can't simply be resubmitted under a new account. If you want this record removed as well, say so in your deletion request and we will remove it.

Anything already sent to an outside service — for example a transcript read by Google (§5) — is held under that service's own retention rules, which we can't shorten.

§11

Security

Every table that holds your data enforces row-level access control, so a signed-in session can only ever read or write its own rows. The fields that make up your verification status are additionally locked to server-side checks — they can't be set directly by the app, only by database logic that verifies the underlying condition itself. Connections between the app and our servers are encrypted.

§12

Age

GPA Sri Lanka is built for enrolled university students. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone below the minimum age to hold a university-issued email address.

§14

What the app's labels mean

The app is careful about a small set of words. This is what each one does and does not claim.

Self-reported
Typed in by the student. Every grade in the calculator, and therefore every GPA on the home screen and on an exported image or PDF, is self-reported — including “what if” grades. Nobody has checked it unless it also says “verified”.
Student-entered
The same thing as self-reported, in the sentence printed at the foot of every export.
Not issued by the university
Printed on every export, verified or not. An export is made by this app from what the student entered. It is not a transcript, certificate or result sheet, and no university produced, approved or signed it.
Estimate
A GPA or classification calculated from the grades entered and the grading scale we hold for that faculty. Your university's own calculation is the one that counts; ours can differ, for example where repeat or minimum-grade rules apply.
Cumulative GPA / Semester GPA
The GPA across every graded module, and the GPA of one semester's modules. Both are estimates from self-reported grades.
Classification
The class (for example First Class or Second Upper) that the estimated GPA falls into, using the university's own thresholds where we hold them and a general Sri Lankan scale where we don't. It is an indication, not an award.
Unverified
A signed-in account that has not linked a university email.
Email verified
The account has linked a Microsoft account whose email address belongs to a university we support. It shows the person controls a university mailbox. It says nothing about their grades.
Verified (profile complete)
Email verified, and the student has entered and locked their name and registration number. Those details are the student's own statement; we have not checked them with the university.
Record checked
The student has submitted a transcript for at least one semester and it passed our automated check: the modules read from it match that degree's published module list. It is our check of a document the student supplied — not confirmation from the university.
“Semester N verified” / “Results verified”
Every module of that semester in the student's checked record came from a transcript that passed the check. On the home screen, “Results verified” means every graded module in the calculator sits in such a semester.
Self-reported · Verified (on an export)
Stricter than the above: every module and grade shown matches, letter for letter, the student's checked record. The grades are still the student's own entries, and the export is still not issued by the university.
Matched to a checked transcript
The sentence used on a verified export, meaning the same as the line above.
Sponsored
On an Opportunities item: the organisation paid to appear on the board.
Partner offer
On an Opportunities item: a commercial offer where we may earn a commission if you use the link. Both labels are stated in words on the card, before you tap.
Guest
Using the app without signing in. Everything stays on the device.
